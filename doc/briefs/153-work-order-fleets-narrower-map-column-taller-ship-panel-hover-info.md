Purpose

Two things on the Fleets screen after Work Order 152 (first line "Work Order 152 — Fleets: function and parity pass"):

The ship panel is too small. Even with font scaling the original's content does not fit comfortably. Data has accepted a new layout.
Hovering over a ship does not show its information in the panel, as it does in the original.
Part A — the new layout

Reference mockup (1440p, accepted by Data): ~/Downloads/fleets_mockup_narrow.png. It is a mockup, not art: the right column in it is simply stretched, and the text is a placeholder font. Build the real thing from the tracked source screens/fleets/assets/_src/fleets_frame_4k_map165.png, reproducibly, through tools/fleets_frame_build.py (or an extension of it).

Target, in 4K source pixels (1440p values x1.5):

Map hole: 195 px lower (1101 -> about 906) and narrower so its aspect stays about 1.6458, like today (about 1491 wide). The galaxy stretch must stay at today's value against 1.265 — report the measured number.
Ship panel hole: 195 px taller. The row of three small boxes moves with it; the left column's top and bottom edges stay where they are.
The whole left column narrower by the width the map loses; the three small boxes and the text box follow the map's new width.
The right column wider by the same amount, taking the freed space. Cells, buttons and the scroll-bar area are rebuilt at their new size, not stretched: corners and chamfers kept at their current pixel size, only straight runs lengthened (the 3-slice method used for Support/Combat in 151). The 4x5 grid fills the wider area; cells may become wider than tall, as in the mockup. The scroll bar keeps its width.
The outer ring is not touched.

Then: new holes cut, boxes regenerated by the existing rule, the kept boxes re-seated and checked for containment (the 151 lesson), the ship panel's text re-laid in two columns at the new width. The frame canvas stays 3840x2160 (decision 70).

Report the measured map aspect, stretch, and the ship panel's line capacity at the current font size at all four resolutions. The longest realistic panel (header lines plus the longer of weapons/specials at the design maximum) must fit at 1440p without the overflow line — say whether it does.

Part B — hover shows the ship's information

In the original, moving the mouse over a ship in the grid shows that ship's details in the panel. In HD it does not. Establish:

what the original shows in the panel when a ship is hovered, when one is selected, and when neither (read the scanner that sets it; recall from 151 B that in Scan_Fltscrn_Big_Icons_ a positive index means hover);
whether HD can build the hovered ship's panel from data it already holds, without sending anything to the game. Prefer that: hover must never cause a send, since the API has no mouse motion and a send per mouse movement would flood the input loop.

Then implement it, including the hover mark from 152 item 5 and the panel changing together.
